# Whole-codebase review, 2026-11-10

Three independent reviewers, one each for accuracy/correctness (A),
speed/efficiency/memory (E) and robustness (R), over all of `anvilgp/` at
`2f66d0a`, the masked feature in most depth. Read-only: nothing below is
fixed yet. "Reproduced" means a script produced the numbers given; the
GPU was shared, so no timing here is a measurement (marked *indicative*).
Earlier review: [review-2026-10-26.md](review-2026-10-26.md).

No wrong maths, index error or dead gradient was found in the masked
kernel path or in the unconvolved likelihood. Every Term's state-space
form matches its analytic kernel to 1e-14. No NaN or flat failure value
reached a sampler in any attack.

**New, found while fixing W2.** A convolved Matern-5/2 on the default
(square-root) boundary is wrong at ordinary parameters, inside the
construction gate: N = 2000, cadence 0.03, exposure 0.02, s/e 10, rho
from 2 down to 0.15 (`||F delta||` 0.07 to 0.9): 1.4e-2 at the reference
point itself, 7.5 log-units at rho = 0.485, against 1e-4 on the dense
boundary and 1e-4 with one block. The Matern terms were put on the
square-root boundary on a *gradient* measurement (boundary-plan.md,
follow-on step 1: dense 1.38 posterior sigma, square-root 0.04); nobody
mapped the value. So neither boundary serves a convolved Matern
everywhere: square-root has the gradient and loses the value, dense the
reverse on bright TESS-like data. **Decided 2026-11-10: back to the
dense boundary** (`_SQRT_CONVOLVED`); the gradient floor on bright data
is the known cost.

## Wrong answers with no warning

**W1. Instrument labels validated against `max(n_jitter, n_offset)`** (R1;
reproduced, and re-confirmed by hand). `likelihood.py:656`. With 2
jitters, 3 offsets and labels 0-2, label 2 indexes past the jitter list:
on the kernel it reads the next chain's row. Three identical chains gave
`[-272.311, -272.311, -271.911]`. Every path agrees, so
`validate_precision` cannot see it. Bites only when 2 <= the smaller
count < the larger. Fix: validate against each list separately.

**W2. Convolved term, sqrt boundary chosen at the reference only** (A1,
R4; reproduced by both). `_convolution_takes_sqrt`, `likelihood.py:3050`;
`_SQRT_FDELTA = 0.5` is tested at the reference, and nothing guards a
chain between there and `MAX_FDELTA = 8`. Convolved SHO, default policy:
exact to `||F delta||` ~ 3.3, then 0.06 at 3.5, 0.4-10 at 4-7, about -110
at 7.3. The dense boundary and the MLX path are right at all of them
(<= 6e-4). On data from an overdamped SHO with `make_target` defaults, 64
draws near the truth: median error 0.05, max 1.23. `boundary-plan.md:877`
states "0.07 at ~2" and nothing further out. Fix: refuse per chain above
~3 under the sqrt boundary, or choose dense when the prior box reaches
that far.

**W3. Convolved term returns the failure value in a band of valid
parameters** (A2; reproduced). `elements.py:289-291`. On the formal path
the leaf takes `rsqrt` and `log` of `s_q = hQh + r`, which can be negative
while K is positive definite (min eigenvalue 0.16) and a sequential
formal filter is exact to 1e-12. Convolved SHO at Q = 0.232: rho 0.09
fine, 0.086 and 0.08 NaN, 0.07 fine. 4 of 64 draws in the W2 realistic
case fell in it. Contradicts `boundary-plan.md:851-856`. Fix: carry the
sign of `s_q` through the rank-one leaf, as `combine_leaf` does for
`alpha`.

**W4. Masked likelihood on the MLX path is inaccurate inside the prior**
(R2; reproduced). `_evaluate_masked`, `likelihood.py:1759`
(information-form fusion). Reached by m > 6, `metal=False`, or a custom
term with no `reversal_signs`. Four SHOs (m = 8), 96 draws within +-2
prior sd: 7 off by > 1 log-unit, worst +77 (optimistic); unmasked, 0.
Over the whole box with `metal=False`: 22-32 of 256 off by > 1 at kappa
19-500, so `kappa_max` does not catch them. A masked convolved SHO was
accurate (0.05-0.12). The plan recorded the mechanism as an excluded
regime; its frequency inside the prior, and the silent m > 6 fallback,
are new.

**W5. Masked float64 `hi` is wrong at large amplitude where the float32
kernel is right** (R3; reproduced against 80-digit mpmath). Same code as
W4. At sigma/yerr ~ 1e6: 8e-5 to 2e-3; ~1e7: up to 0.12; ~1e8: up to 8.8;
beyond, NaN, finite garbage (-1.9e43 passes `_sanitize`) or an MLX
`triangular inversion failed`. The kernel stays <= 4e-4 throughout. Far
outside any posterior, but `hi` is what `validate_precision` and
`reanchor_every` trust. One fix serves W4 and W5: the covariance
two-filter form on the MLX/float64 path.

**W6. Masked kernel near `kappa_max` at m >= 4** (R5; reproduced). kappa
3e3-7e5, ~4 prior sd out in sigma: Rotation +30 (regular) and +126
(irregular) against 0.6-0.8 unmasked; SHO+SHO -23 against 0.46. Single
m <= 3 terms stay <= 0.6. `kappa_max = 1e6` was calibrated unmasked.

**W7. Masked + marginalised basis loses 10-100x of float32 accuracy when
the window is at an end of the data** (A4; reproduced; rounding, not
logic). The repo test's own data with a leading window: value 4.6e-4 to
1.4e-3 against a tolerance of 3e-4; RealTerm gradient 6.8e-4 against
1e-4. A basis column concentrated in a leading window gave one
mean-parameter gradient the wrong sign. Interior windows pass.

**W8. `t`, `y`, `yerr` are aliased, not copied** (R7; reproduced).
`likelihood.py:547-549`. Mutating `y` afterwards moves `hi` and `predict`
but not the float32 value.

**W9. Bad inputs accepted** (R8; reproduced). Float instrument labels
truncated before validation; extra `ParamMap` kernel entries ignored;
`linear_prior` without a basis ignored; a NaN reference or basis column
makes every chain the failure value with no construction error.

Possibly known: unmasked SHO+SHO across a gap at kappa 7e4 is +-15-28 off
on the kernel (R6); not checked against the floors in float32.md.

## Crashes

- **C1.** Masked gradient on the MLX path with many windows exhausts
  Metal's buffer count; a convolved term with 150 windows at N = 6000
  aborts the interpreter (R9). `_evaluate_masked` loops over segments in
  Python. The same loop makes masked `hi` cost ~27 ms per window
  (E12, *indicative*): about a minute per 64 chains at 2000 windows.
- **C2.** `predict` at held-out times raises for a convolved term with a
  basis: the internal M points collide with the caller's (A3).
- **C3.** `predict(v, [])` raises `KeyError: 'mean'` (R10).
- **C4.** `deepcopy` fails once a chunked front end is cached (R11).

## Speed and memory

Cheap and safe:

- **E1.** The reversed masked scan covers kept points no window reads:
  42% of them on the benchmark layout. Estimated 15-20% of masked cost
  (2.2x/2.5x -> ~1.9x/2.0x at 1024 chains); needs a quiet timing.
- **E2.** Checkpoint buffer for placed blocks sized `n_blocks x
  ceil(block/SLEN)`: 75 MiB where 4 are needed at 200 windows (measured);
  12.6 GiB per direction on paper at N = 1e5, 2000 windows, 1024 chains.
- **E3.** `_lag_fold_plan` falls to one scatter pair per block with many
  windows on an irregular cadence: 1604 scatters per gradient at 200
  windows (measured plan), 16,000 at 2000.
- **E4.** `hi` and float64 `gram` with a basis still polarise: 448 lanes
  at p = 2, 4288 at p = 10 (measured); `linear_posterior` gets the same
  Gram in one pass. Est. 3x to 20x.
- **E5.** `periodogram_period`: 8.9 s and 643 MiB at N = 20000
  (*indicative*), ~45 s at 1e5.
- **E6.** Per-call host work on the masked path (`kappa` front end, index
  arrays in `window_walk`).

Needs a redesign or an accuracy re-run:

- **E7.** `Phi C Phi^T` as an m^4 sum in the forward step and backward
  sweep: an m^3 form was ~1.3x faster at m = 6 (*indicative*, patched in
  memory), nothing at m <= 3. Bears on the tracked m = 6 item.
- **E8.** `bwd_ckpt` pass 1 repeats the forward replay (~10% of v+g).
- **E9.** A full r-sensitivity pass per direction for the masked VJP.
- **E10.** Window walk is sequential in window length.
- **E11.** No memory fence on the masked gradient; the walk stores every
  point's state twice (9 GiB each on paper at 4096 chains x 14,000 held
  out, m = 4).
- **E13-14.** Residual/basis gathers; `_metal_tables` row-0 concatenation.

## Errors that arrive late or say the wrong thing

`mean_fn` / `linear_basis` of the wrong shape or type, a `KernelParam`
index out of range, a wrong-sized `linear_prior`, string labels,
`n_jitter=2` without `instrument`, `hi_chain_block=0`, `metal=0`: all
fail at the first call with an MLX or NumPy message, not at construction.
`GPPolicy` accepts `block=0`, `metal='yes'`. Full list in the robustness
report.

## Docs that disagree with the code

- Tutorial: `gram`, `linear_posterior`, `predict` "work unchanged" when
  masked, then `apply_inverse` with no caveat; it refuses a mask.
- `noise.scale_and_jitter` docstring: jitter range "the same span as the
  default" (1e-3 to 10 against 1e-3 to 1e3).
- masked-gp-plan: 7.6e-4 at scale is one layout; 2.2e-3 to 4.0e-3 at
  N = 6000 with three windows.
- `TermConvolution`: "exact everywhere" in float64; up to 1.3e-4 near the
  series edge.

## Not reached

`special.py` function by function; anything at N >= 1e5 or 4096 chains on
the GPU; long sampler runs; evidence functions; most benchmark numbers
